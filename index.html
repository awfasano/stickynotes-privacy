Sticky Notes — Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Sticky Notes is a visionOS productivity app built by Waited Co. We believe a notes app should stay out of your business. This policy explains what that means concretely.

What we collect

Nothing.

Sticky Notes does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app does not include analytics, advertising SDKs, crash telemetry, user accounts, or any network requests to our servers. Your notes, sketches, timers, and focus-session history live entirely on your Apple Vision Pro.

What stays on your device

  • Your sticky notes (text, checklist items, and sketches)
  • Timer state and focus-session history
  • App preferences (sort order, widget setup status)

All of this data lives in the app's local sandbox and its shared App Group container (so the widget extension can read pinned notes). Nothing leaves the device.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Sticky Notes schedules a local notification when a timer completes. These notifications are generated entirely on your device. We never see them.

Widgets

The Sticky Notes widget extension reads pinned notes from a shared file in the App Group container on your device. It never communicates with any server.

Third-party services

Sticky Notes does not integrate with any third-party services.

Children's privacy

Because Sticky Notes collects no personal data, it does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction).

Changes to this policy

If we change what data the app collects, we will update this page and, if the change is material, we will bring it to your attention inside the app before it takes effect.
